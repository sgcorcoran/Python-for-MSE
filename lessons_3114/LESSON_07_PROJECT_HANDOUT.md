# Lesson 07: Practical Research Project - Quick Overview

**Duration**: 2 weeks (Weeks 11-12) | **Time**: 3 hours per week

---

## What You'll Do

Apply all the skills you've learned (Lessons 01-06) to a **real materials science research question**. You'll choose from **three project options** and work through a **structured, guided process** with clear milestones.

---

## Project Options (Choose ONE)

- **Option A**: Analyze relationships between alloy composition and properties
- **Option B**: Identify optimal processing conditions from experimental data  
- **Option C**: Classify materials based on property data

---

## What Makes This Manageable

✅ **Structured milestones** - Clear checkpoints throughout (not left on your own!)  
✅ **Step-by-step guidance** - Templates and checklists provided  
✅ **Validation checkpoints** - Use Lesson 05 strategies to catch errors early  
✅ **Flexible data options** - Use previous lesson data, provided datasets, or public databases  
✅ **Realistic scope** - 3 hours/week, quality over quantity  
✅ **Builds on familiar skills** - Uses everything you've already learned  

---

## Deliverables

- Project Notebook (analysis with validation checkpoints)
- Technical Report (3-4 pages)
- Workflow Documentation (AI usage log)

*Detailed requirements and grading rubrics will be in the lesson notebook.*

---

**Don't worry!** This project is designed to be achievable and rewarding. You'll have full support and clear structure throughout. The detailed lesson notebook will provide all the guidance you need.
