## 🎯 Quick Overview
Apply all skills (Lessons 01-06) to a real materials science question. Use AI tools, validate outputs, document workflow, create professional report.

---

## 📋 Choose ONE Project

| Option | Research Question | Key Skills |
|--------|------------------|------------|
| **A: Composition-Property** | How do alloy compositions (Cu, Mg, Si) relate to mechanical properties? | Correlation analysis, scatter plots |
| **B: Processing Optimization** | What are optimal heat treatment conditions (temp, time) for target properties? | Group comparison, statistical tests |
| **C: Material Classification** | Can materials be classified by properties? What properties distinguish types? | Property comparison, distribution analysis |
