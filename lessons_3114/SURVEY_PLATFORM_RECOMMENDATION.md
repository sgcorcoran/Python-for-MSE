# Survey Platform Recommendation: Microsoft Forms

## Recommendation: Microsoft Forms ✅

### Why Microsoft Forms for Your Situation

#### 1. **Integration with Your Existing Setup**
- ✅ You're already using **OneDrive** (files are in OneDrive)
- ✅ VT students likely have **Microsoft 365 accounts** (VT provides Office 365)
- ✅ **Easy access** - students can access via VT Microsoft account
- ✅ **Single sign-on** - students already signed in (but form can still be anonymous)

#### 2. **Ease of Setup**
- ✅ **Faster to set up** - already in Microsoft ecosystem
- ✅ **No additional accounts needed** - uses your VT Microsoft account
- ✅ **Built into Office 365** - accessible from VT portal
- ✅ **Familiar interface** - likely similar to other Microsoft tools you use

#### 3. **Anonymity Features**
- ✅ **True anonymity** - can disable name collection completely
- ✅ **"Anyone with link"** option works well
- ✅ **No authentication required** for respondents
- ✅ **Complies with FERPA** for educational use

#### 4. **Analysis & Data**
- ✅ **Built-in analytics** - visual summaries automatically generated
- ✅ **Easy export to Excel** - great for detailed analysis
- ✅ **Response summaries** - see patterns quickly
- ✅ **Individual response review** - see each response (anonymously)

#### 5. **Distribution**
- ✅ **Share via Canvas** - easy integration
- ✅ **QR code option** - quick in-class distribution
- ✅ **Direct link** - simple URL to share
- ✅ **No login required** - students don't need extra accounts

#### 6. **VT-Specific Benefits**
- ✅ **VT IT support** - your IT department can help if needed
- ✅ **Already approved** - likely approved for educational use
- ✅ **FERPA compliant** - educational privacy compliance built-in
- ✅ **No additional costs** - part of VT's Microsoft 365

## When to Consider Google Forms

**Choose Google Forms if:**
- ❌ Your VT doesn't provide Microsoft 365
- ❌ You prefer Google ecosystem

**For your situation:** None of these apply → **Microsoft Forms is better**

- ✅ Automatic distribution
- ✅ No external links

- ⚠️ **Check anonymity settings** before using
